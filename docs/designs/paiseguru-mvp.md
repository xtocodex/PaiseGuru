## Problem Statement
Young Indians make many small UPI payments (assumption: 20+ a week; to be checked in the Assignment interviews) and share many costs (rent, trips, dinners) with friends. Two tools cover two halves badly: Splitwise tracks who owes whom but not what you personally spent; budget/SMS trackers count the full ₹3,000 dinner as your spend and ignore the ₹2,250 friends owe you back. Nobody gets an honest "what did I actually spend this month" number.

PaiseGuru v1: one entry, two ledgers. A group expense is split once; your share lands in your personal budget, the rest becomes a receivable, and settle-up happens over a UPI link.

Second problem (founder's own family, added after review): several siblings jointly support a dependent parent. The founder's grandmother depends on her 4 sons. All month her costs (medicines, food, accessories, other items) are paid by whichever son is at hand. At month-end the total is divided among the sons, not always equally: sometimes one son gives a fixed extra amount and the rest is divided equally, then whoever paid more than his share is paid back and the month is "settled and cleared". Today this is worked out by hand in a notebook ("noted in copy"). Splitwise does not fit: it splits each expense when it is added and keeps a never-ending running balance, while this family needs expenses to pile up unsplit and be divided once, with a custom rule, at a monthly close. PaiseGuru v1 adds this as a second group type, **Monthly Hisaab**, and it is the product's USP.

### Approach C: Local-first sync (Zero / ElectricSQL)
Rejected: heavy new infrastructure before the first user; harder permission rules for shared money.
### API framework: Hono over Express 5
Founder asked. Chosen for the end-to-end typed client (the frontend fails to compile when a route or response shape changes), a one-line better-auth mount (web-standard Request), and runtime portability. ## Product Spec v1 (single source of truth)
This section replaces the earlier Money Rules, Monthly Hisaab Rules, CEO Review Additions and SMS Capture Rules. Every worked example here is a required test fixture.

### S1. Money basics
- All stored amounts are integers in paise and are non-negative: expense totals, shares, entries, transfers, settlements, deposits. Derived values are signed integers in paise: balances, nets, opening balances, pool balance.
- Every API money field is named `*Paise` and validated by zod as an integer at the boundary. Bounds: one entry or expense ≤ ₹1,00,00,000 (10⁹ paise); a sheet or group ≤ 100 members; a weight ≤ 100 shares (10,000 hundredths). `allocate()` computes with BigInt internally, so products like total × weight can never lose precision.
- Balances, nets and spend are derived at read time from stored rows. No running balance is stored.
- Time: all dates and month boundaries are in IST (Asia/Kolkata).

### S2. The allocation function (one function for every split)
`allocate(totalPaise, participants[{weight}], order) → sharePaise[]`
- Each share = floor(total × weight / sum of weights). The leftover paise go one each by largest fractional remainder; ties are broken by `order`.
- `order`: the payer first (when the payer is a participant), then members by member-row creation time (a placeholder keeps its creation time after being claimed).
- Equal split = every weight 1. Example: ₹100 / 3 = ₹33.34 + ₹33.33 + ₹33.33 (payer gets the extra paisa).
- Weights are stored as positive integers in hundredths (1 share = 100) and all arithmetic is integer: share = floor(total × w / Σw), remainder by largest `(total × w) mod Σw`.
- A split (one expense, or one Hisaab sheet's remainder) is in exactly one mode: **Shares** (every participant has a weight, default 1 share; ratios like 1.5 allowed) or **Percent** (every participant has a percent with up to 2 decimals and they total exactly 100; stored as hundredths of a percent). Mixing percent and shares in one split is not allowed.
- Invariant: shares sum exactly to the total.
- Shares are recomputed only when an expense's amount, participants or weights change.

### S3. Members, placeholders, joining, leaving
- A member is a real user or a placeholder (name only). Any member can add placeholders.
- Invite link: random 128-bit token; no expiry; any member can regenerate it, which revokes the old link. A joiner (once approved, where approval is on) sees the group's full history.
- Every group and Hisaab has one **admin** (the creator at first). The admin can hand the role to another real member. The admin cannot leave or be removed while other real members remain unless the role is handed over first; if the admin's account is deleted, the role passes to the oldest remaining real member.
- On join: the joiner picks "I'm <placeholder name>" from the unclaimed placeholders, or "I'm new". The admin can undo a wrong claim, and can link an existing member to an unclaimed placeholder, merging the placeholder into that member. Merge rules: shares of both on the same expense are added together; balances between the two are dropped; the merge is blocked while an unpaid transfer between the two exists. Claimed placeholders' past shares count in the claimant's personal spend for their original months.
- Leaving or removing: any member can remove a member, and a member can leave, only when that member has, in this group: every pairwise balance at zero (split-as-we-go); and no entries, no unpaid transfers and a zero opening balance in any Open or Closed sheet (Hisaab).
- Removed members become "former members": they keep read-only access to expenses and sheets they took part in. Expenses involving a former member can no longer be deleted or change amount, participants or weights.
- Account deletion (the user deletes their account from Me): their member rows in every group and Hisaab become "Deleted user" members that keep all amounts, shares, entries and transfers so no one else's numbers change; their profile, UPI ID, personal expenses, personal ledger, budget and templates are deleted; photos they attached to personal entries are purged; photos on group or Hisaab entries stay with those shared records. Admin roles they held pass on as above.
- Join approval: a Hisaab has "Admin approves new members" on by default (split-as-we-go groups: off by default). Until approved, a joiner sees only the group name. Bill photos are visible only to approved members.
- Recurring templates whose payer or a participant is removed pause themselves and show "A member left; update this template".

### S4. Split-as-we-go groups
- An expense has a total, one payer (who need not be a participant), participants and a split: Equal, Exact amounts (must sum to the total, otherwise save is blocked with "₹X left to assign"), or By shares (weights).
- Category is set per expense and applies to every participant's share.
- Balances are pairwise between two members within a group. No debt simplification.
- Edit and delete: any current member may edit or delete a group expense (soft delete). Every create, edit and delete writes an activity row. Refunds: edit or delete the original expense (no refund entries here in v1).
- Duplicates: on save, if an expense in this group involving the same payer has the same amount within ±2 days, show "Looks like <expense> already exists" with **Attach** or **Save anyway**. Attach discards the new entry; if the new entry had a photo it is added to the existing expense; it never changes the existing amount, payer or shares.

### S5. Hisaab projects and sheets
- A Hisaab has: name, members, optional "For" label (e.g. "Dadi"; never a paying member, never needs an account), a budget category for members' ledger lines (default "Shared"), a type (Monthly or One-time), and for Monthly a cycle start day D (1–28, default 1).
- Monthly sheets run from day D to day D−1 of the next month. A sheet's **month** is the calendar month it starts in; it names the sheet ("September", or "September: 5 Sep – 4 Oct" when D ≠ 1) and is where members' ledger lines post (S7). Every sheet starts in the calendar month after the previous sheet's month, so each month has exactly one sheet.
  - Start month: chosen at creation, default the current month, up to 12 months back so old notebook months can be back-filled. The first sheet starts on day D of that month (a Hisaab created on 17 Sep with D = 1 and start month September gets 1 Sep – 30 Sep). All earlier sheets up to today open at creation.
  - Entries dated before the first sheet's start are rejected with "This Hisaab starts on <date>".
  - Changing D applies from the next sheet not yet opened. That transition sheet starts at the old boundary and ends the day before new day D of the following calendar month. Examples: old D = 5, new D = 25: transition 5 Oct – 24 Nov (October), then 25 Nov – 24 Dec (November). Old D = 25, new D = 5: transition 25 Oct – 4 Nov (October), then 5 Nov – 4 Dec (November).
  - Sheets are opened by the daily job on their start date (S14), and also on demand by any write or close that needs the next sheet. Unique key (Hisaab, start date) prevents duplicates.
- One-time Hisaab: a single sheet with no cycle dates, closed whenever members are ready, never rolled over. Carry is not offered. Recurring templates are not allowed. Adding entries after close needs a reopen.
- Sheet states: **Open** → **Closed** (pending payments) → **Cleared** (all transfers paid) or **Carried** (balances moved to the next sheet). A reopened sheet returns to Open.

### S6. Hisaab entries
Entry types (all amounts stored positive):
- **Bill**: amount, category, date, note, "Paid by" (a member, "Outside the split", or "From pool" when a pool is on). Outside-the-split bills show in the total but are excluded from the dividable total.
- **Refund**: reduces the dividable total; "Received by" a member or the pool.
- **Money given**: member to member, mid-month.
- **Deposit**: member to pool (pool on).
- **Withdrawal**: pool to member (pool on).
- **Brought forward**: opening balance lines created by a carry (S7); never counts as spend; not editable.

Placement: several sheets can be Open at once (e.g. September stays Open on 3 Oct until the family closes it). An entry belongs to the sheet whose date range contains its date if that sheet is Open. If that sheet is Closed, Cleared or Carried, the entry goes into the latest Open sheet (opening it on demand), unless the admin reopens the older sheet. Its accounting month is then that Open sheet's month (it is split and posted with that sheet); its original date is kept only as a label, shown as "Late: dated 28 Sep". Entry dates after today are rejected. A sheet can only be closed when every earlier sheet is Closed, Cleared or Carried.

Permissions: any current member may add, edit or soft-delete entries in an Open sheet, including entries whose payer is another member or a placeholder (one person can manage everything). Each change writes an activity row. Brought forward lines are created and removed only by the system.

Duplicates: same type and amount within ±2 days in the same Hisaab shows **Keep both** or **Discard**. Settlement and transfer duplicates follow S8.

Common pool (optional Hisaab setting "Use a common pool"). Turning it on requires choosing a **pool holder**, who must be a member (changeable later, activity row). Optional pool UPI ID (e.g. the beneficiary's bank account VPA) and label (e.g. "Dadi's bank account"); without a pool UPI ID, top-ups go to the holder's UPI ID. The holder is the person who physically holds the pool money:
- Pool balance = pool opening balance + deposits − bills paid from pool − withdrawals + refunds received by the pool. Shown all month. Negative is allowed and shows "Pool is short ₹X".
- The pool can be turned on at any time (it starts at ₹0 on the Open sheets). It can be turned off only when every Open sheet has a pool balance of ₹0, a pool opening balance of ₹0 and no pool entries.
- A pool can only be funded by member deposits. Money from outside (e.g. the beneficiary's own savings) is recorded as "Outside the split" bills, not as pool money.

### S7. Hisaab close, settle plan, carry
Close can be started by any member on or after the sheet's last day (Monthly) or any time (One-time). The person who started confirms.

Sheet participants: each sheet has its own participant list, taken from the members when the sheet opens. A member who joins while a sheet is Open is added to it with the Skip exception pre-set (visible, and any member can change it before close). A placeholder claim keeps the placeholder's place. Reopening a sheet whose participants include a former member is blocked ("<name> has left this Hisaab").

Close screen: total, category breakdown, what each member paid, pool balance if any, and each member's resulting share. By default every participant splits equally and the UI shows no "equal" label. Per-member exceptions for this sheet, shown as small tags and pre-filled from the previous sheet ("Clear exceptions" resets them):
- **Fixed ₹X**: pays exactly ₹X and is left out of the split of the remainder.
- **₹X extra**: pays ₹X plus a share of the remainder.
- **Weight** (e.g. "2 shares" or "40%"): share of the remainder by weight. A sheet's remainder is in one mode (S2). Default: Shares, everyone 1 share. Typing a percent for any member switches the whole sheet to Percent mode and pre-fills every remainder member's percent from their current shares, rounded so they total exactly 100; switching back to Shares restores 1 share each. The mode and values are pre-filled from the previous sheet like other exceptions.
- **Skip**: pays nothing this sheet.

Formula:
- Dividable total = bills paid by members + bills paid from pool − refunds. (Outside-the-split bills excluded.)
- Remainder = dividable total − sum of Fixed and Extra amounts.
- Remainder shares = allocate(remainder, members not Fixed or Skip, by weight).
- Obligation = Fixed amount, or Extra amount + remainder share, or remainder share, or 0 for Skip.
- Close is blocked with a clear message if: Fixed + Extra exceed the dividable total; remainder > 0 and no member is left to share it; or the dividable total is negative.
- Examples: total ₹20,000, member A Fixed ₹8,000 → the other 3 pay ₹4,000 each. A ₹8,000 extra → all 4 have ₹3,000 of the remainder; A's obligation ₹11,000.

Nets:
- Member net = opening balance + bills they paid + deposits + money given − money received − withdrawals received − refunds received − obligation.
- Pool holding = pool closing balance. Invariant: sum of member nets = pool holding (0 when no pool).
- Settle plan: parties are the members plus the pool (pool net = − pool holding). Greedy: largest debtor pays largest creditor, repeat. At most (parties − 1) transfers. Transfers can be member→member, member→pool ("top up the pool": Pay button uses the pool UPI ID, else the holder's) or pool→member ("pool pays back": made by the holder from the pool money; Pay button shown to the holder).
- Worked example (fixture): pool on, opening 0; deposits Ramesh ₹10,000 and Suresh ₹5,000; bills from pool ₹12,000; Mahesh paid a ₹3,000 bill himself. Dividable ₹15,000, 3 members, ₹5,000 each. Nets: Ramesh +5,000, Suresh 0, Mahesh −2,000; pool holding ₹3,000. Check: 5,000 + 0 − 2,000 = 3,000. Plan: Pool pays Ramesh ₹3,000; Mahesh pays Ramesh ₹2,000.
- Worked example (fixture, no pool): total ₹20,000; paid Ramesh ₹9,500, Suresh ₹7,000, Mahesh ₹2,500, Dinesh ₹1,000; Ramesh Fixed ₹8,000. Obligations 8,000 / 4,000 / 4,000 / 4,000. Nets +1,500 / +3,000 / −1,500 / −3,000. Plan: Dinesh → Suresh ₹3,000; Mahesh → Ramesh ₹1,500.

Zero-activity sheet: if a sheet has no entries AND every opening balance and the pool balance are ₹0, closing goes straight to Cleared. Otherwise the normal close runs.

Concurrency: every change to a sheet (entries, exceptions, close, reopen, carry, marking a transfer paid) checks and increments the sheet's version number in the same database transaction. A request made against an old version is refused with "This month changed, review again", so a transfer can never be both paid and carried.

Close snapshot (D5): the close writes a snapshot of its computed result (dividable total, obligations, nets, pool holding, settle plan) in the same transaction. Closed, Cleared and Carried sheets display from the snapshot; reopen deletes it. A test asserts that recomputing a closed sheet from its rows equals its snapshot, so a later code change can never silently alter a settled month (a mismatch is logged as an error and the snapshot is shown).

After close: entries and exceptions are read-only. Each transfer is marked paid by either party; pool transfers can be marked paid by the pool holder or by the other member in that transfer. Each mark writes an activity row. Partial payment: the payer may mark a smaller amount paid; the rest stays as an unpaid transfer. When all transfers are paid the sheet is **Cleared**.

Carry (Monthly only):
- **Carry to next month** at close: no transfers; each member's net becomes that member's opening balance on the next sheet and the pool holding becomes the next sheet's pool opening balance. The sheet shows **Carried**. Invariant on every sheet: sum of member opening balances = pool opening balance.
- **Carry the unpaid ones** on a Closed sheet: converts only the unpaid transfers into opening balances on the next sheet (opening it on demand); paid transfers stay. For each unpaid transfer of ₹T from X to Y: X's opening balance −T, Y's opening balance +T; when X or Y is the pool, that side goes to the pool opening balance with the opposite sign (pool pays Ramesh ₹T → Ramesh +T, pool opening +T). The sheet shows **Cleared**.
- Worked example (fixture, mixed): the pool example's plan, with "Pool pays Ramesh ₹3,000" paid and "Mahesh pays Ramesh ₹2,000" unpaid, carried: next sheet opening balances Ramesh +2,000, Mahesh −2,000, Suresh 0, pool opening 0 (2,000 + 0 − 2,000 = 0).
- Carry is blocked if the next sheet is already closed.
- Worked example (fixture): the pool example above, carried: next sheet opening balances Ramesh +5,000, Suresh 0, Mahesh −2,000, pool opening ₹3,000 (5,000 + 0 − 2,000 = 3,000).

Reopen (Hisaab admin only; activity row): blocked if the next sheet is closed ("Reopen the later month first"). Otherwise: paid member-to-member transfers become Money given entries, paid member→pool transfers become Deposits, paid pool→member transfers become Withdrawals. These converted entries always belong to the reopened sheet (their payment date is kept for display only). Unpaid transfers are deleted; any Brought forward lines this sheet created on the next sheet are removed. No recorded payment is ever lost.

Personal ledger: at the first close, each member gets one ledger line "<Hisaab name> – <sheet name>" equal to their obligation, in the Hisaab's category, posted to the sheet's month (S5) (One-time: the month of the first close; a later reopen and re-close keeps that month and updates the amount). Before close, Home shows "<Hisaab> – <sheet> so far: ₹X (not closed yet)", computed with the current exceptions, under the sheet's month. This estimate is shown only; it does not count toward spend or the budget until the close posts the ledger line. Transfers, deposits, withdrawals, money given and brought-forward lines never count as spend.

Hisaab invariants (tests): obligations sum to the dividable total; member nets sum to pool holding; settle plan transfers exactly clear all nets; a closed sheet's numbers do not change until reopened; reopening never loses a paid transfer; opening balances sum to the pool opening balance.

### S8. Settlements (split-as-we-go groups)
- Recorded per group between two members, by either of them. Amount: positive paise, default the full amount owed, may be less (partial). More than owed needs a confirm ("This is more than you owe") and flips the balance.
- No counterparty confirmation. Either party can delete a mistaken settlement. Create and delete each write an activity row.
- Duplicate prompt: same two members, same amount within ±2 days → **Keep both** or **Discard**.
- `via`: `upi_link` or `manual`.
- Pay button (settlements and Hisaab transfers): `upi://pay?pa=<UPI ID>&pn=<name>&am=<rupees>&cu=INR&tn=PaiseGuru <group>`. Always also offered: "Copy UPI ID + amount" and "Save QR image" (for scan-from-gallery or another phone). If the payee has no UPI ID, the Pay button is hidden, "Ask <name> to add a UPI ID" is shown, and marking paid still works.
- UPI ID check: `^[a-zA-Z0-9.\-_]{2,256}@[a-zA-Z][a-zA-Z0-9]{1,63}$`.
- The app never receives payment confirmation from UPI apps; marking paid is always a manual tap.

### S9. Personal ledger and budget
- Personal monthly spend = your personal expenses + your shares of split-as-we-go expenses + your Hisaab ledger lines, by expense date (or the sheet's month) in IST. Settlements, transfers and brought-forward lines never count.
- One monthly budget total with a category breakdown (no per-category limits).
- Categories (fixed enum, no custom categories in v1): Food, Groceries, Housing, Household help, Utilities, Transport, Travel, Shopping, Entertainment, Healthcare, Education, Personal care, Insurance, Gifts, Bills, Family support, Shared, Other. AI guesses must be one of these, else Other. The Year view totals by these categories; questions like "how much on medicines" are answered by the Healthcare total or by searching notes.

### S10. Sharing
- WhatsApp: `wa.me` links with plain-text messages. "Tell the group" after adding an expense; "Remind" on settle-up; "Share on WhatsApp" after a Hisaab close (sheet name, total, category totals, each member's paid and obligation, transfers, and the statement link). Kept short; the full list is at the link.
- Statement link: a random 128-bit token for one sheet, read-only, no login, showing every entry and the calculation. Shows names and amounts only, never UPI IDs or emails. Any member can revoke it and create a new one.
- Photos on statement links: the "Include bill photos" flag belongs to the token. Turning it on creates a new token that expires after 30 days. Photos are served to a valid token only while its flag is on (S13 proxy); revoking or expiry ends access immediately.

### S11. SMS capture
- Parser output: amount as written (e.g. "Rs.250.00"), direction (debit or credit), merchant or VPA, date, category guess, `isTransaction`.
- Before the LLM call the server masks: account-number digits, reference numbers, and any currency figure within 20 characters after a balance or limit keyword ("Avl Bal", "Available", "Bal", "Balance", "Limit", "Lmt", "Avl Lmt").
- The server converts the amount to paise with a deterministic parser and accepts it only if it appears verbatim as a currency amount in the raw text and is not a masked balance figure. Otherwise the amount field is left blank.
- Not a transaction, parse error, or timeout (5 s): the manual form opens with any parsed fields and "Couldn't read this SMS, please check the fields". Nothing auto-saves.
- Credit SMS: offer "Record as a settlement received from…" (with the S8 duplicate prompt) or discard. No income tracking.
- A debit the user marks as a settlement goes to settle-up.
- Destination picker on the prefill screen: Just me (personal expense), a split-as-we-go group, or a Hisaab (as a Bill paid by you). The Hisaab option says "Hisaab bills count in your spend only through the month-end share", so one UPI debit is never counted twice.
- Privacy: raw SMS is never stored. The provider must be configured for no training on the data. Screens say "This text is sent to our AI to read it; we don't store it".
- Limits: signed-in only; 30 AI calls per user per day shared with bill scan (S12); 1,000 characters max.
- Signed out when an SMS is shared: the text is kept in localStorage with a 15-minute expiry through Google sign-in, then cleared when the prefill screen opens.
- Fixtures must include SMS with several rupee figures (amount plus balance plus limit).

### S12. AI bill scan
- "Scan bill" on any add screen: take or pick a photo; it is attached (S13) and a vision call prefills amount, merchant, date and category, plus a short item summary in the note for multi-item bills.
- Unlike SMS there is no independent text to check against, so the check is only for plausibility: the amount must be a well-formed currency figure and not smaller than the largest line item the model returns. The user always confirms the amount; nothing auto-saves; failure opens the manual form.
- Flow: the photo is uploaded first as a draft (S13); the server fetches it from storage and sends it to the vision model.
- Privacy: same no-training provider terms as S11. The scan screen says "This photo is sent to our AI to read it".
- Shares the 30-per-day AI limit.

### S13. Bill photos
- Up to 5 images per entry. The browser re-encodes each to a JPEG with a 1600 px long edge (about 300 KB), which also strips EXIF data such as GPS. Files over 10 MB before re-encoding are rejected with a message.
- Storage: private bucket. Upload uses a short-lived pre-signed PUT URL with the Content-Type (image/jpeg) and Content-Length signed in, issued only to someone allowed to add or edit entries in the target scope (a member of that group or Hisaab, or the owner for personal entries); before the entry exists the upload is a draft, stored as a server-side row (uploader, scope, object key). A draft can be attached only by its uploader, only to an entry in the same scope; attach requests with any other key are refused. After upload the server checks the object (HEAD) and deletes anything over 2 MB or not image/jpeg.
- Viewing: photos are streamed through the API (`GET /api/photos/:id`), which checks permission on every request: members of that group, the owner of a personal entry, or a valid statement token with photos on (S10). No storage URL is ever given to the browser, so revoking a token or removing a member stops access immediately.
- Deleting an entry hides its photos at once; the daily job removes the files after 30 days. Draft uploads never attached to an entry are removed after 24 hours.

### S14. Recurring entries and the daily job
- A template: scope (personal expense, split-as-we-go expense, or Monthly Hisaab bill), amount, category, note, payer, split (for groups), and day of month 1–31. Days 29–31 in a shorter month run on the last day.
- Keys: personal and group templates fire once per calendar month (key: template + month). Hisaab templates fire on every date within a sheet that matches their day (key: template + date), so a long transition sheet can hold two and a short one none. Days 29–31 use the last day of that date's calendar month.
- Entries are created on their due day (not in advance). Created entries are normal entries marked "Recurring" (source `recurring`), editable or deletable for that period only. Editing a template changes future periods only. Templates can be paused and ended.
- Daily job (in-process scheduler, 00:05 IST, also run once at server start), in order: (1) open every Monthly Hisaab sheet whose start date is today or earlier and is not yet open; (2) create every recurring entry due on or before today with no instance yet, only for due dates on or after the later of the template's start date and its last resume date (catch-up after downtime; nothing is created for dates before a template existed or while it was paused). A caught-up entry gets its due date and is placed by the S6 placement rule (its sheet if Open, else the latest Open sheet with a "Late" label); (3) purge photos deleted more than 30 days ago and draft uploads older than 24 hours. Every step is idempotent, enforced by unique constraints on (Hisaab, sheet start date) and on the template keys, and the whole job runs under a Postgres advisory lock so two server instances during a deploy cannot run it at once.

### S15. Activity and "New since your last visit"
- Activity rows are written for: expense create, edit, delete; settlement create, delete; Hisaab entry create, edit, delete; close, reopen, carry; transfer marked paid; placeholder claim, undo, link; member join, leave, remove.
- Home shows the activity involving you since your last visit, newest first.

### S16. Year view and CSV
- Hisaab Year view: per sheet total, category totals, and each member's paid and obligation, for any 12-month window.
- CSV export (generated in the browser from an API response): Hisaab entries for a date range, and your personal ledger for a date range. Columns: date, type, description, category, amount (rupees, 2 decimals), paid by, your share (blank for Hisaab rows), source. Only data the user can already see.
- Any cell starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is prefixed with `'` so spreadsheets never run it as a formula.

### S17. Authorization
- Enforced on every API route: group and Hisaab data (entries, settlements, balances, invites, activity, photos, templates) for current members only, plus read-only access for former members to items they took part in; personal data for its owner only; statement tokens only for their one sheet. Cross-group access returns 404 and has tests.

### S18a. Feature switches (D6)
- Server settings `AI_SMS`, `AI_SCAN`, `PHOTOS` (default on). When one is off, its buttons are hidden, the API refuses its calls with a clear message, and manual entry keeps working. Changing a switch needs only a config change and restart, no code.

### S18. Metrics
- Every expense and Hisaab entry records `source` = the creator's channel: `manual`, `sms_share`, `sms_paste`, `bill_scan`, `recurring`, plus `createdBy`. Settlements and transfers record `via`.
- Metrics are SQL queries: entries per group per week; per user, "created by me via channel X" vs "created by someone else"; settle-ups by `via`; Hisaab sheets closed and cleared per month.

Findings:
- OK: domain/ is pure (no DB, no clock, no randomness), so every money rule is unit-testable and later reusable by a native app.
- OK: one process, one database, one bucket. Coupling is justified at this size.
- WARNING (scaling): balances are derived at read time over all rows. Fine to thousands of entries per group; the first thing to slow down is the Year view and group balances on long-lived groups. Remedy (D5, accepted): close snapshots; closed sheets display from them.

- Rollback: redeploy the previous build (minutes). Migrations are additive (expand, then contract in a later release), so old code runs against the new schema.
- Platform potential: domain/ + the typed API are the platform; a native Android app (SMS auto-capture) reuses both unchanged.

```

 read v=7                       read v=7                         7
 tx: check v=7 ✓, paid, v=8                                      8
```

## Reviewer Concerns

### R2-1 — consistency

**Problem**

> Ledger invariant (4) says 'every money value is a non-negative integer paise', but derived balances are signed by design (Home shows Office lunch -₹300, and 'Goa Trip +₹900 = ... − Aman ₹600'), so the invariant as written is false for balances and would fail its own Vitest test.

**Remedy**

> Scope invariant (4) to stored amounts (totalPaise, sharePaise, settlement amount are non-negative integers) and state that derived balances are signed integer paise.

### R2-2 — consistency

**Problem**

> The Assignment still lets the build proceed with 6 yeses out of 15 ('Fewer than 6 yeses out of 15 means revisit the wedge'), while P6 and Success Criteria require at least 10 named people onboarded; passing the gate with 6-9 yeses leaves the success criterion unreachable unless more people are recruited, which the document does not plan.

**Remedy**

> Either raise the go threshold to 10 yeses (or say the founder keeps recruiting until 10 before launch), or restate the success criterion relative to the number who said yes.

### R2-3 — completeness

**Problem**

> Settlements can be recorded or deleted by either party with no counterparty confirmation, which changes your balance, but 'New since your last visit' (feature 9) lists only expenses others added, edited or deleted, and an activity row is committed only for settlement deletes, not creates. A counterparty can silently mark a debt settled.

**Remedy**

> Write an activity row for every settlement create and delete, and include settlements (and placeholder claims/undos) involving you in 'New since your last visit'.

### R2-4 — completeness

**Problem**

> The duplicate check covers expenses only. A credit SMS can be turned into 'Record as a settlement received from…' after the payer has already tapped 'mark settled', producing two settlements and a wrong balance.

**Remedy**

> Apply the same duplicate prompt to settlements (same two members, same amount, within ±2 days) with Attach or Save anyway.

### R2-5 — completeness

**Problem**

> Partial settlements are allowed, but there is no amount validation: it is not stated whether a settlement must be positive, whether it may exceed the amount currently owed, and what happens to the balance direction on overpayment.

**Remedy**

> State that a settlement amount is a positive integer paise and either cap it at the current pairwise amount owed or explicitly allow overpayment that flips the balance.

### R2-6 — clarity

**Problem**

> `source` is stored per expense but its values mix capture channel with viewer perspective: `group` means 'created by someone else', which differs per member for the same expense. It is unclear what is stored when you create a group expense manually or from SMS, so the 'share of personal expenses by source' metric is ill-defined.

**Remedy**

> Store only the creator's channel (`manual`, `sms_share`, `sms_paste`) plus the creator ID, and define the metric as 'created by me via channel X' vs 'created by someone else' computed per user in the query.

### R2-7 — clarity

**Problem**

> The duplicate prompt offers 'Attach (no new entry)', but what Attach does is undefined: whether it changes the existing expense (e.g. sets payer, source, or merchant), links the SMS to it, or simply discards the new entry.

**Remedy**

> Define Attach precisely (e.g. discards the new entry and, if the existing expense has no capture source for you, records that it was confirmed via SMS; never changes amount, payer or shares).

### R2-8 — feasibility

**Problem**

> SMS rules require the server to mask 'balance figures' before the LLM call and a 'regex cross-check on the raw text' that must agree with the LLM amount, but bank SMS often contain several rupee figures (amount, available balance, limits). Masking balances and the cross-check both require telling the transaction amount apart from the balance, the exact problem the LLM was added to solve, and the agreement rule is undefined.

**Remedy**

> Define the rules: mask figures anchored to balance keywords (e.g. 'Avl Bal', 'Bal', 'available'), and accept the LLM amount only if it appears verbatim as a currency amount in the raw text and is not a masked balance figure; cover multi-figure SMS in the fixtures.

### R2-9 — completeness

**Problem**

> 'A member can be removed only when their balance in that group is zero' does not say who can remove a member (creator, any member) or whether a member can leave a group on their own.

**Remedy**

> State the actor for removal and whether self-leave exists (with the same zero-balance rule).

### R2-10 — completeness

**Problem**

> Balances are pairwise with no debt simplification, but the removal rule checks 'their balance in that group' without saying whether that is the net balance or every pairwise balance. A member owing A ₹100 and owed ₹100 by B has zero net but real outstanding debts.

**Remedy**

> Require every pairwise balance of the member within the group to be zero before removal.

### R2-11 — completeness

**Problem**

> After removal, the removed member's past shares still count in their personal spend, yet authorization limits group expenses to current members and any current member may edit or delete those expenses. The removed member loses sight of entries in their own ledger, and later edits can change their spend and make their balance non-zero with no way to settle.

**Remedy**

> Decide one: removed members keep read access to expenses they participate in, or expenses involving a removed member become read-only (no edits or deletes that change their share).

### R2-12 — clarity

**Problem**

> The equal-split leftover order is 'payer first, then by the order they joined the group', but placeholders never join; it is unclear whether a placeholder's position is its creation time or its claim time, and whether an edit to an old expense recomputes shares with a changed order.

**Remedy**

> Define the order on a stable key that exists for placeholders (e.g. member row creation time, unchanged by claiming) and state that shares are recomputed only when the expense's amount or participants are edited.

### R2-13 — completeness

**Problem**

> Merchant refunds (credit SMS from Amazon, Swiggy, etc.) reduce what you actually spent, but v1 only offers 'Record as a settlement received' or discard, and invariant (4) forbids negative amounts, so the honest-spend number overstates spend after refunds. This limitation is not stated.

**Remedy**

> Either add refunds to the explicit NOT-in-v1 list with the guidance to edit or delete the original expense, or support a refund that reduces the original expense.

### R2-14 — clarity

**Problem**

> Categories are committed (per expense, budget breakdown by category, LLM 'category guess'), but the category set is never listed; the old app's list is cited only as reference. Zod validation of the parser output and the budget breakdown need a fixed enum.

**Remedy**

> List the v1 categories (or state that the old app's list is adopted as-is) and require the LLM category guess to be one of them, falling back to 'Other'.

### R2-15 — feasibility

**Problem**

> Shared SMS text is held in sessionStorage through Google sign-in, but in an installed Android PWA the out-of-scope OAuth navigation may complete in a different browsing context (custom tab or the browser), where sessionStorage from the PWA window is not available, so the shared text could still be lost.

**Remedy**

> Verify the flow on an installed Android PWA, or hold the text in localStorage/IndexedDB with a short expiry and clear it once the prefill screen opens.

### R2-16 — feasibility

**Problem**

> The QR code fallback is shown on the payer's own phone, which cannot scan its own screen; it only helps if the payer saves it and uses the UPI app's 'scan from gallery', or shows it to another device. The document presents it as a general fallback without that step.

**Remedy**

> Add a 'Save QR image' action and say the QR is for scan-from-gallery or another device, or drop the QR and rely on 'Copy UPI ID + amount'.

### R2-17 — consistency

**Problem**

> The wireframe's Settle up screen states 'Opens GPay / PhonePe with amount filled' as fact, while the Open Questions say whether UPI apps open a P2P `upi://pay` link from a PWA with the amount filled is unverified and must be checked in Hour 1.

**Remedy**

> Mark that wireframe caption as the expected behavior pending the Hour 1 check, or reword it neutrally (e.g. 'Opens your UPI app').

### R2-18 — consistency

**Problem**

> The wireframe does not show committed v1 UI: the SMS prefill screen lacks the required notice 'This text is sent to our AI to read it; we don't store it', Home lacks 'New since your last visit', and Add expense lacks the 'Tell the group' WhatsApp nudge.

**Remedy**

> Update the wireframe to include these elements, or state that the wireframe predates them and the text spec takes precedence.

### R2-19 — completeness

**Problem**

> Wrong placeholder claims can be undone by the creator, but a joiner who picks 'I'm new' when they were actually a placeholder has no path to be linked to that placeholder later; their past shares stay on the placeholder and never reach their personal spend.

**Remedy**

> Let the group creator link an existing member to an unclaimed placeholder (merging the placeholder's shares), or state that this case requires re-entering those expenses.

Close additionally reads entries after that update (the row lock is held until commit), so no entry can slip in between preview and close. Required proof of an approved contract.
